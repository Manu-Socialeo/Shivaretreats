Ashram Rules & Code of Conduct

An ashram is a sacred vessel of transformation. To uphold the purity, peace, and spiritual focus of our community, all students and guests are requested to honor the following guidelines.

1. Substance-Free Environment (Shaucha)

Shiva Yoga is a strictly alcohol-free, drug-free, non-smoking, and non-meat environment. Bringing, consuming, or storing non-prescribed intoxicating substances on the ashram premises is strictly prohibited to preserve high vibrational clarity.

2. Punctuality & 100% Attendance

Students registered in Yoga Teacher Training courses must maintain 100% attendance in all scheduled asana, anatomy, philosophy, and pranayama lectures to qualify for Yoga Alliance USA certification. Please arrive at the Yoga Shala 5 minutes before class begins.

3. Sacred Silence & Quiet Hours (Mauna)

To support deep inward reflection and restful sleep, silence is observed across the ashram grounds from 10:00 PM until 08:00 AM. Please keep mobile devices on silent mode in all common spaces, dining areas, and yoga shalas.

4. Modest & Respectful Attire

Please wear modest, comfortable, and respectful attire around the ashram, dining room, and during traditional puja ceremonies. Swimwear is restricted strictly to the pool area and beach.

5. Seva & Respect for Nature

We take joy in living in harmony with nature. Please respect all animals, medicinal herbs, and fellow ashram residents. Practice conscious water and electricity conservation during your stay.
