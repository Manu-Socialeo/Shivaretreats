Privacy & Data Policy

How we protect and respect your personal information at Shiva Yoga Goa.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a yoga teacher training course, book a retreat, or contact us, we collect information including your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, nationality, date of birth, dietary preferences, and relevant medical/injury history to ensure your safe practice.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to process your reservation, arrange airport pick-up and accommodation, issue your Yoga Alliance certificate, and provide important pre-arrival course updates. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties.

3. Photography & Media Release

During courses and retreats, photos and videos of group classes may be taken for educational and promotional use. If you prefer not to appear in media, simply notify our ashram manager upon arrival and your privacy will be strictly respected.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at contact@shivaretreats.com or call +91 74119 24193.
