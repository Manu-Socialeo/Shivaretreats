The Sacred Energy of the Himalayas

Dharamshala (home to His Holiness the Dalai Lama) is one of the most spiritually charged places on Earth.

Cool Mountain Climate

Pleasant 20°C–26°C weather throughout May to September, surrounded by cedar forests and fresh glacier streams.

Tibetan & Vedic Culture

Deep spiritual confluence of classical Indian yoga philosophy and Tibetan Buddhist meditation practices.

Himalayan Nature Treks

Weekly guided mountain walks to Triund peak, Bhagsu waterfall, and ancient Himalayan temples.

Same Yoga Alliance Credential

Identical international 200hr / 300hr / Yin syllabus taught by our permanent faculty from Goa.
