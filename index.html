A Day in the Life

The Sacred Daily Ashram Schedule

Our immersive daily routine is designed in accordance with classical Dinacharya (yogic circadian rhythm) to cultivate focus, energy balance, and lifelong transformation.

06:30 AM – 07:30 AM

Pranayama, Shatkarma & Cleansing Kriyas

Awaken the breath and purify the respiratory passages with traditional breathwork and cleansing rituals.

07:30 AM – 08:30 AM

Vedic Mantra Chanting & Meditation

Calm the mind and elevate vibrational frequencies through sacred Sanskrit sound resonance.

08:45 AM – 10:15 AM

Morning Dynamic Asana (Hatha / Ashtanga)

Build physical strength, flexibility, and alignment through structured posture sequencing.

10:15 AM – 11:15 AM

Nourishing Sattvic Breakfast

Fresh tropical fruits, Ayurvedic herbal porridge, fresh juices, and organic plant-based delicacies.

11:30 AM – 01:00 PM

Yoga Philosophy, Anatomy & Alignment

Study Patanjali’s Yoga Sutras, Bhagavad Gita, functional bio-mechanics, and posture adjustments.

01:15 PM – 02:15 PM

Ayurvedic Sattvic Lunch & Rest

Balanced, warm yogic meal followed by personal integration, study, or relaxing by the pool.

04:15 PM – 05:45 PM

Evening Vinyasa Flow / Yin & Teaching Practicum

Refine hands-on teaching methodology, student adjustments, and gentle restorative flows.

07:30 PM – 08:30 PM

Satsang, Sound Healing or Ecstatic Dance

Evening community gatherings, kirtan music, Tibetan singing bowl sessions, and sacred silence.

Everything you need to know about preparing for your journey to Shiva Yoga in Goa.

Arambol in North Goa is renowned worldwide as a spiritual and creative hub for yogis, healers, and seekers. Surrounded by lush coconut groves, gentle ocean breezes, and golden beaches, it offers a peaceful haven away from tourist crowds, allowing you to immerse completely in self-discovery and study.

Yes, Shiva Yoga is a Registered Yoga School (RYS 200, RYS 300, RYS 500) with Yoga Alliance USA. Upon successful completion of our courses, you receive an internationally recognized certificate allowing you to register as an RYT and teach anywhere in the world.

We serve 3 freshly cooked, 100% vegetarian and vegan-friendly Sattvic Ayurvedic meals every day. Our ingredients are locally sourced and prepared with Ayurvedic herbs to support digestion, energy, and mental clarity during rigorous yoga practice.

Goa is served by two international airports: Manohar International Airport (MOPA - GOX), which is only 40 minutes away, and Dabolim Airport (GOI), which is approximately 90 minutes away. We provide convenient direct airport taxi pickups for all our students.
