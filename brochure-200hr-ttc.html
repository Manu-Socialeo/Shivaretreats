This 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course in Arambol, Goa is a transformative 21-day residential journey designed for passionate practitioners and aspiring yoga teachers. Curriculum & Core Learning Modules

1. Asana Practice & Sequencing Mastery

In-depth study of 80+ primary and intermediate Hatha and Ashtanga Vinyasa postures. Master safe alignment, anatomical checkpoints, props integration, intelligent sequencing, and modifications for all body types.

2. Pranayama, Bandhas & Subtle Energetics

Master sacred breath techniques: Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Ujjayi, Sitali, Sheetkari, and Bhramari. Practical activation of Mula, Uddiyana, and Jalandhara Bandhas, Prana Vayus, Nadis, and the 7 Chakras.

3. Functional Yoga Anatomy & Physiology

Skeletal, muscular, and nervous system dynamics during asana. Joint biomechanics, spinal health, fascia, connective tissue safety, and prevention of yoga-related injuries.

4. Yoga Philosophy, Ethics & Yogic Lifestyle

Study of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras (8 Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga), Yamas & Niyamas, Bhagavad Gita insights, Karma Yoga, and establishing an ethical student-teacher relationship in modern yoga studios.

5. Teaching Practicum & Voice Modulation

Hands-on teaching drills starting from Week 1. Real-time class delivery, cueing, time management, student observation, adjustments, overcoming teaching anxiety, and Yoga Alliance registration guidance.

Travel & Packing Guide

How to Reach Arambol, Goa

• MOPA Airport (GOX): 35–40 mins (28 km) drive.

• Dabolim Airport (GOI): 85–90 mins (60 km) drive.

• Train: Pernem (20 mins) / Thivim (35 mins).

• Taxi Pickups: Available on request with our trusted ashram drivers.

Things to Bring

  • Comfortable yoga wear + white attire for ceremonies
  • Refillable water bottle (UV/RO filtered water on tap)
  • Personal toiletries, sunscreen & mosquito repellent
  • Universal international power adapter (Type C/D)
  • Notebook, journal & pens for lectures
Ashram Rules & Code of Discipline
  • 100% Substance-Free: Strictly vegetarian, smoke-free, alcohol-free, and drug-free sanctuary.
  • Outside Curfew: All students must return inside the ashram premises by 10:30 PM for campus security.
  • Quiet Hours: Lights off at 10:00 PM; silence is maintained in dormitories to support deep rest.
  • Attendance Requirement: Minimum 90% attendance is required to receive Yoga Alliance USA certification.
Check-in & Cancellation Policy

• Check-in & Check-out: Check-in is at 10:00 AM on the 2nd; Check-out is by 2:00 PM on the 22nd (Includes 1 complimentary extra night).

• Deposit & Transfer: Deposits are non-refundable but 100% transferable to future dates within 24 months.

• Payment Fees: PayPal 5% surcharge; Card swipe 3%; Cash accepted in INR.
