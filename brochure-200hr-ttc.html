About This Experience

This 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course in Arambol, Goa is a transformative 21-day journey designed for passionate practitioners and aspiring yoga teachers. Guided by traditional Indian yoga masters, you will establish a solid foundation in classical asanas, pranayama, functional anatomy, alignment, subtle energy, and Vedic philosophy.

Our curriculum synthesizes traditional Hatha Yoga, dynamic Ashtanga Vinyasa, and modern teaching methodologies, empowering you with the practical tools and global credentials (200 RYT) to teach with depth, authenticity, and confidence anywhere in the world.

Curriculum & Core Learning Modules

1. Asana Practice & Sequencing Mastery

In-depth study of 80+ primary and intermediate Hatha and Ashtanga Vinyasa postures. Master safe alignment, anatomical checkpoints, props integration, intelligent sequencing, and modifications for all body types.

2. Pranayama, Bandhas & Subtle Energetics

Master sacred breath techniques: Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Ujjayi, Sitali, Sheetkari, and Bhramari. Practical activation of Mula, Uddiyana, and Jalandhara Bandhas, Prana Vayus, Nadis, and the 7 Chakras.

3. Functional Yoga Anatomy & Physiology

Skeletal, muscular, and nervous system dynamics during asana. Joint biomechanics, spinal health, fascia, connective tissue safety, and prevention of yoga-related injuries.

4. Yoga Philosophy, Ethics & Yogic Lifestyle

Study of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras (8 Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga), Yamas & Niyamas, Bhagavad Gita insights, Karma Yoga, and establishing an ethical student-teacher relationship in modern yoga studios.

5. Teaching Practicum & Voice Modulation

Hands-on teaching drills starting from Week 1. Real-time class delivery, cueing, time management, student observation, adjustments, overcoming teaching anxiety, and Yoga Alliance registration guidance.

How to Reach Arambol, Goa

• From Manohar International Airport MOPA (GOX): Located in North Goa, just 35–40 minutes (28 km) drive to our Arambol ashram.

• From Dabolim International Airport (GOI): Approximately 85–90 minutes (60 km) drive via NH 66.

• By Train: Pernem Railway Station is 20 minutes away; Thivim Railway Station is 35 minutes away.

• Ashram Taxi Pickups: Direct private taxi pickups are available on request with our trusted ashram drivers. Please inform admissions at least 3 days prior to your arrival date.

• Exact Campus Address: Shiva Yoga Retreats & TTC, Arambol Beach Road, Pernem, North Goa 403519, India.

200-Hour TTC • Rules, Policies & Contact Shiva Yoga Goa
Ashram Rules & Code of Discipline
  • 100% Substance-Free Sanctuary: Strictly vegetarian, smoke-free, alcohol-free, and non-drug environment at all times.
  • Outside Curfew: If leaving the ashram premises during free hours, all students must return by 10:30 PM for campus security.
  • Quiet Hours: Lights off at 10:00 PM; silence is maintained in accommodation dormitories to support deep rest and rejuvenation.
  • Attendance & Certification: A minimum of 90% class attendance is mandatory to receive your official Yoga Alliance USA certificate.
Deposit & Cancellation Policy

1. Advance Deposit: A registration deposit is required to confirm your seat. Course deposits are non-refundable but 100% transferable to any future batch or date within 24 months.

2. Course Balance: The remaining course balance must be cleared upon arrival at check-in.

3. Payment Surcharges: PayPal transfers incur a 5% processing surcharge; Card swipe terminals incur 3%. Cash payments are accepted in Indian Rupees (INR) only.
