Curriculum & Core Modules

1. Asana Practice & Sequencing: Master fundamental to intermediate Hatha and Ashtanga Vinyasa postures with deep understanding of anatomical alignment, modifications, contraindications, and prop usage.

2. Pranayama & Breath Science: Master Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Ujjayi, Sitali, Sheetkari, and Brahmari breath control along with the 3 primary Bandhas (Mula, Uddiyana, Jalandhara).

3. Yoga Anatomy & Physiology: Study skeletal, muscular, and nervous systems, joint mechanics, fascia, subtle body anatomy (7 Chakras, Nadis, Koshas, and Prana Vayus).

4. Yoga Philosophy & Ethics: Deep dive into the 8 Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga (Yamas & Niyamas), Bhagavad Gita, Hatha Yoga Pradipika, and ethical codes for modern yoga teachers.

5. Teaching Practicum: Practical teaching drills from Week 1, voice modulation, class sequencing, cueing, time management, and hands-on adjustments under master supervision.
