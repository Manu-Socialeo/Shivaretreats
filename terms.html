Terms & Conditions

Please review our booking guidelines, deposit structures, and lifetime date-transfer flexibility.

1. Advance Booking Deposit

To secure your room and reserved seat in a Yoga Teacher Training Course or Retreat, a non-refundable advance deposit of €300 (or equivalent) is required upon registration. The remaining balance of the course fee is payable upon your arrival at the ashram via cash (EUR / USD / GBP / INR) or bank wire.

2. Lifetime Date Rescheduling Guarantee

We understand that unexpected life events, flight changes, or health concerns may arise. While deposits are non-refundable, your advance deposit is 100% transferable for a lifetime (up to 24 months) to any future course or retreat date at Shiva Yoga Goa upon at least 15 days written advance notice.

3. Course Attendance & Certification

Graduation and issuance of the Yoga Alliance USA certificate are contingent upon full course completion, active participation, and adherence to the ashram code of conduct.

4. Health & Medical Responsibility

Students are responsible for ensuring they are in adequate physical and mental health to undertake rigorous physical training. Please notify faculty of any pre-existing injuries or medical conditions prior to commencing classes.
