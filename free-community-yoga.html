Our Philosophy

Why We Offer Free Community Yoga

In traditional yogic culture, Seva (selfless service) is considered one of the highest spiritual practices. Shiva Yoga was founded on the belief that spiritual tools should be accessible to everyone — travelers on a budget, local villagers, seekers, and students alike.

Our community classes are taught by senior Indian master teachers and passionate visiting teachers. Every session includes classical Asana, Pranayama, and guided Meditation in our breezy open-air shala surrounded by palm trees.

No pre-registration is needed. Simply bring a mat, an open heart, and your curiosity.

What to Expect in Community Classes

An authentic, grounding, and joyful yogic practice for everyone.

Opening Mantras

Centering the mind and aligning the group energy with traditional Vedic chanting.

Gentle & Dynamic Flow

A balanced blend of classical Hatha alignments and fluid Vinyasa transitions.

Pranayama Breathing

Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, and Sheetali breathwork to regulate the nervous system.

Sound & Relaxation

Deep Savasana with Tibetan singing bowls and gentle aromatherapy.

Community Yoga FAQ

Do I need to book in advance?

No advance booking is required. Simply arrive 10 minutes before 9:00 AM to set up your mat.

Is it really completely free?

Yes! It is completely free. We have a donation box at the entrance if you wish to contribute towards tea and community ashram maintenance, but donations are 100% voluntary.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely. Our teachers provide variations for all levels from complete beginners to advanced practitioners.

Do I need to bring my own yoga mat?

We have complimentary yoga mats, blocks, and straps available in the shala, but you are welcome to bring your own personal mat.
