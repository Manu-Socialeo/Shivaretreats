Important Travel Information & Goa Guide

We want your arrival and stay in Goa to be as effortless and peaceful as possible. Here is your essential guide to flights, visas, climate, and packing.

1. Arriving in Goa (Airports & Transfers)

Goa currently has two operational airports:

  • Manohar International Airport (MOPA / GOX): Located in North Goa, only 35–45 minutes drive from our ashram in Arambol. This is the most convenient airport to fly into.
  • Dabolim Airport (GOI): Located in Central Goa, approximately 75–90 minutes drive to Arambol.

Airport Pickup: We arrange direct private taxi pickups for our students. Our trusted driver will hold a name sign at the arrivals gate and bring you straight to the ashram doors.

2. Indian Visa (e-Visa)

Most international travelers can easily apply for an Indian e-Tourist Visa (30-day, 1-year, or 5-year) online via the official Government of India portal (indianvisaonline.gov.in).

We recommend applying at least 2–3 weeks prior to your departure date. Please ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity from your date of arrival and at least two blank pages.

3. Comprehensive Packing Checklist

Recommended to Bring:

  • ✓ 4–6 sets of lightweight yoga clothes
  • ✓ Modest clothing for temple & pujas (white attire optional for graduation)
  • ✓ Swimwear & beach towel
  • ✓ Refillable water bottle
  • ✓ Personal journal & pens
  • ✓ Natural mosquito repellent & sunscreen
  • ✓ Universal power adapter (Type C / D / M)

Provided by the Ashram:

  • ✓ High-density yoga mats & props
  • ✓ Yoga blocks, straps & bolsters
  • ✓ Complete printed course textbooks
  • ✓ Shatkarma cleansing kit & Neti pot
  • ✓ Bed linen, pillows & hot water
  • ✓ Filtered UV drinking water stations

4. Money, SIM Cards & Climate

  • Currency: Indian Rupee (INR). ATMs and currency exchange counters are widely available throughout Arambol. International credit cards are accepted in many places, but having cash for small local purchases is recommended.
  • SIM Cards & Internet: Local Airtel / Jio SIM cards with generous 5G data packages can be purchased at the airport or local shops with a passport copy. The ashram provides complimentary high-speed WiFi.
  • Goa Seasons: October to April offers glorious sunny days (28°C–32°C / 82°F–90°F) with pleasant ocean breezes and clear starry nights—the premier yoga season in India.
