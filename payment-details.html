Payment & Deposit Guidelines

Clear, transparent deposit instructions for reserving your TTC or Retreat spot at Shiva Yoga Goa.

How Booking Works

  1. Advance Deposit: A deposit of €200 / $220 / ₹15,000 is required to confirm and guarantee your seat in any TTC program. For retreats, the deposit is €100.
  2. Remaining Balance: The remaining course fee is payable upon arrival at the ashram check-in via cash (EUR, USD, GBP, INR), Wise, or card.
  3. Transfer Confirmation: Once you make the deposit, kindly share a screenshot or transaction reference with us via WhatsApp at +91 74119 24193 or email contact@shivaretreats.com.
