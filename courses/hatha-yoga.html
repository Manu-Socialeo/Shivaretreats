Classical Vedic Roots

Traditional Hatha Yoga Immersion in Goa

Harmonize the solar and lunar energies within. Experience authentic classical Hatha asanas, extended posture holds, internal mudras, and purification kriyas in peaceful Arambol, Goa.

The Classical Foundation of All Physical Yoga

Hatha Yoga is the mother of all physical yoga disciplines. Rooted in the ancient Sanskrit scriptures Hatha Yoga Pradipika and Gheranda Samhita, Hatha uses physical postures (Asanas), breath restraint (Pranayama), internal seals (Mudras), energy locks (Bandhas), and purifications (Shatkarma) to prepare the body for Samadhi.

Unlike modern fast-paced fitness hybrids, our classical Hatha immersion emphasizes steady, steady-minded holds (Sthira Sukham Asanam), precise anatomical alignment, and cultivation of deep inner stillness.

Curriculum & Practice Focus

1. 84 Classical Asanas

Master primary, intermediate, and restorative Hatha postures with steady alignment and breath awareness.

2. Shatkarma Kriyas

The 6 purification techniques: Jala Neti (nasal wash), Sutra Neti, Trataka (gazing), and Nauli (abdominal churning).

3. Hatha Texts Study

Chapter-by-chapter exploration of the Hatha Yoga Pradipika and the philosophy of vital energy (Prana).

4. Mudras & Bandhas

Khechari, Viparita Karani, Maha Mudra, and the 3 great bandhas for energetic redirection.
