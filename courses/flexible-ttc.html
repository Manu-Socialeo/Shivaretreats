100-Hour Flexible Modular Yoga TTC in Goa

Can't take 3 weeks off all at once? Complete the first 11 days (100 hours) now and return anytime within 24 months to complete your full 200-Hour Yoga Alliance certification.

Flexibility Designed for Busy Lifestyles

We understand that taking 21 continuous days away from work or family commitments can be challenging. Our 100-Hour Modular TTC allows you to complete Part 1 (Foundations of Hatha, Ashtanga, Pranayama & Philosophy) during an 11-day stay in Arambol, Goa.

Upon completion, you receive a certified 100-Hour Certificate of Completion. Whenever you are ready, you can return to complete Part 2 (Teaching Methodology, Adjustments & Advanced Practicum) to receive your full 200 RYT Yoga Alliance credential.

Part 1 Curriculum Focus

Foundational Asana

Standing, seated, twisting, and forward folding postures with precise biomechanical alignment.

Pranayama Fundamentals

Diaphragmatic breathing, Kapalabhati, Nadi Shodhana, and gentle morning Shatkarma cleanses.

Vedic Philosophy

The history of Yoga, Yamas & Niyamas, intro to Patanjali’s Sutras, and mind management.

Personal Practice Deepening

Daily immersion with seasoned Indian yogis to overcome personal physical and mental blocks.
