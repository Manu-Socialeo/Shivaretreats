Rejuvenating Yoga Retreats in Goa (3, 5, 7 Days)

Step away from the noise and restore your inner balance in the lush coastal beauty of Arambol, Goa. Beachside yoga, healing breathwork, ice baths, and nourishing Sattvic cuisine.

Nourish Your Body, Calm Your Mind, Awaken Your Spirit

Our Goa Yoga Retreats are crafted for anyone seeking deep rest, holistic rejuvenation, and personal renewal. Whether you join for a long weekend (3 days), a restorative reset (5 days), or a full week of transformation (7 days), you will be held in a supportive ashram sanctuary surrounded by tropical palm trees and soothing sea breezes.

What You Will Experience

Beach & Shala Yoga

Daily morning and evening sessions blending gentle Hatha, dynamic Vinyasa, and deeply calming Yin Yoga.

Tibetan Sound Healing

Sunset sound journeys with Tibetan singing bowls, gongs, and chimes to dissolve nervous system tension.

Ice Bath & Mud Rituals

Boost immunity, reduce inflammation, and enhance mental resilience with guided cold exposure and volcanic mud cleanses.

Organic Sattvic Nutrition

3 fresh, chef-prepared Ayurvedic vegetarian meals daily using local produce and digestion-boosting herbs.
