50-Hour Yin Yoga Teacher Training in Goa

Explore the meditative depth of stillness. A 6-day certified immersion into myofascial release, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) meridians, and mindful Yin sequencing.

Balance the Yang with the Sacred Yin

In our fast-paced modern world, the cooling, grounding practice of Yin Yoga provides the essential counterpart to dynamic Yang practices (Vinyasa and Ashtanga). By applying gentle, prolonged stress to the deep connective tissues (fascia, ligaments, and tendons), Yin stimulates the flow of Chi / Prana through subtle energy meridians.

This 50-hour course is certified through Yoga Alliance YACEP (Continuing Education) and provides teachers and dedicated students with a profound anatomical and energetic toolkit.

Curriculum Highlights

Fascia & Connective Anatomy

Understand viscoelasticity, creep, plastic deformation, and skeletal variation in joints.

TCM Meridian Theory

The 12 primary organ meridians, 5 Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), and Chi flow.

Prop Mastery & Modifications

Creative usage of bolsters, blocks, blankets, and sandbags for therapeutic accessibility.

Yin Sequencing & Holding

How to hold space, cue stillness, modulate silence, and build thematic meridian sequences.
