Ancient Healing Science

6-Day Ayurvedic Wellness & Detox Retreat in Goa

Cleanse toxic accumulation, balance your Vata, Pitta, and Kapha Doshas, and learn time-tested Ayurvedic lifestyle secrets in tranquil Arambol, Goa.

Awaken the Body's Natural Healing Intelligence

Ayurveda is India's 5,000-year-old sister science to Yoga. While Yoga focuses on spiritual liberation and physical vitality, Ayurveda provides the blueprint for optimal cellular health, digestive strength (Agni), and longevity.

In this 6-day residential retreat, you receive personal pulse diagnosis (Nadi Pariksha) by an experienced Ayurvedic physician, customized herbal therapies, daily rejuvenating body massages, and hands-on workshops in Ayurvedic cooking and self-care.

Therapeutic Inclusions

1. Doctor Consultation & Dosha Map

In-depth assessment of your bodily constitution and tailored lifestyle/herbal prescription.

2. Daily Ayurvedic Massages

Warm medicated herbal oil Abhyanga, herbal steam baths (Swedana), and Shirodhara for deep relaxation.

3. Ayurvedic Cooking Classes

Learn the medicinal use of 6 tastes (Shad Rasa), digestive spices, and custom recipes for your Dosha.

4. Daily Rejuvenating Yoga

Gentle therapeutic asanas and calming breathwork specifically aligned with the Ayurvedic detox process.
