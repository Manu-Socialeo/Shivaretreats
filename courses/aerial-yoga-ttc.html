Master Aerial Hammock Yoga & Inversion Therapy

Aerial Yoga uses soft fabric hammocks suspended from the ceiling to support your bodyweight. This enables zero-compression spinal decompression, deeper stretches, core strengthening, and effortless inversions without neck strain.

Our 50-hour teacher training course gives you complete confidence in hammock safety, carabiner and daisy chain rigging, student spotting techniques, progressive aerial sequencing (beginner to intermediate), and restorative floating relaxation (Floating Savasana).

Whether you are adding aerial yoga to your studio offerings or deepening your personal acrobatic practice, this course is safe, empowering, and immensely fun.

6-Day Aerial Curriculum Breakdown

From fundamental rigging safety to advanced aerial flows and restorative yoga.

Day 1
Rigging, Equipment & Safety

Hammock fabric maintenance, carabiners, swivels, load weights, ceiling anchors, height adjustments, and spotting protocols.

Day 2
Ground-Supported & Standing Poses

Aerial Warrior series, hamstring openers, side body stretches, and utilizing the hammock for enhanced alignment and balance.

Day 3
Inversions & Spinal Decompression

Inverted butterfly, aerial star, hip wraps, floating downward dog, and therapeutic benefits for lumbar and cervical spine.

Day 4
Aerial Conditioning & Core Strength

Upper body pulling strength, aerial plank variations, dynamic flips, and transitions that build graceful strength.

Day 5
Restorative Aerial & Sound Cocoon

Low-hammock yin yoga, floating meditation, deep sensory cocooning, and nervous system down-regulation.

Day 6
Teaching Practicum & Graduation

Class sequencing design, teaching complete 60-min aerial classes with hands-on adjustments, final evaluation, and certificate ceremony.
