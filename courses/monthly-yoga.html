30-Day Ashram Living

1-Month Ashram Yoga Living & Immersion in Goa

Make Shiva Yoga your home in Goa. Experience an entire month of unlimited yoga classes, morning pujas, organic vegetarian dining, pool access, and tranquil community living in Arambol.

Slow Down, Reconnect, and Flourish

If you seek a meaningful long-term sabbatical, creative retreat, or spiritual immersion without the pressure of an exam, our 1-Month Ashram Living Program is ideal.

You receive full access to all open morning and evening yoga classes (Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin, Kundalini), daily meditation and pranayama, sound baths, ice baths, 3 nourishing Sattvic meals every day, high-speed WiFi for remote working, and opportunities for selfless community seva.

Included Monthly Benefits

Unlimited Class Access

Attend any drop-in yoga, breathwork, and meditation class on the ashram timetable throughout the month.

90 Sattvic Meals Included

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner cooked fresh daily with organic vegetables, legumes, and Ayurvedic teas.

Digital Nomad Friendly

Quiet garden workspaces, shaded verandas, swimming pool, and high-speed fiber internet.

Community & Karma Yoga

Optional volunteering with our medicinal herb garden, rescued animals, and charity community events.
