About This Course

The Art & Science of Guiding Pregnancy Through Yoga

Pregnancy is one of the most powerful transformations a human being can undergo. This 6-day intensive course equips yoga teachers — or anyone passionate about supporting mothers — with the full toolkit to teach safe, nurturing, and empowering prenatal yoga classes.

Conducted by experienced senior teachers at our Arambol beachside ashram in Goa, this course blends traditional Hatha yoga philosophy with modern evidence-based prenatal guidelines.

Batches run every Monday to Saturday, so you can join any week that suits your schedule.

Course Highlights

  • Safe asanas for all 3 trimesters
  • Pranayama & breathing for labour
  • Yoga Nidra & deep relaxation
  • Postnatal recovery sequences
  • Contraindications & modifications
  • Sequencing prenatal classes
  • Meditation & emotional support
  • Certificate upon completion

6-Day Curriculum Breakdown

Each day builds systematically from foundations to advanced hands-on teaching practice.

Day 1
Foundations & Anatomy

Physiology of pregnancy, trimesters, hormonal changes, contraindications, pelvic floor anatomy, and creating a safe space for pregnant students.

Day 2
First Trimester Practice

Gentle asanas for early pregnancy, managing morning sickness, fatigue yoga, and the do's and don'ts of teaching first-trimester students.

Day 3
Second Trimester Practice

Standing poses, hip openers, balance work, and progressive sequences for the most active phase of pregnancy — safely modified and joyful.

Day 4
Third Trimester & Birth Prep

Squatting sequences, breathing for labour (Lamaze & Ujjayi), positions for birth, comfort techniques, and Yoga Nidra for deep rest.

Day 5
Postnatal Recovery Yoga

Pelvic floor restoration, diastasis recti awareness, new mother sequences, baby yoga, and emotional support frameworks for postpartum.

Day 6
Teaching Practicum

Students teach complete 60-minute prenatal classes, receive feedback, and complete their certification assessment. Graduation & celebration.

Who Should Attend?

Already a Yoga Teacher

Add a highly sought-after specialization to your teaching portfolio and open doors to teaching at maternity clinics, women's wellness centers, and corporate prenatal programs.

Doulas & Midwives

Deepen your birth support practice with practical yoga tools for your clients. Many doulas find prenatal yoga transforms the quality of support they can offer.

Healthcare Professionals

Nurses, physiotherapists, and OBGYNs seeking integrative approaches to recommend to their pregnant patients will find this course invaluable.

Expectant Mothers

If you are pregnant and want a deep dive into safe yoga practice, this course will give you profound tools for your own pregnancy, birth, and recovery journey.
