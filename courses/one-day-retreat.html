1-Day Yoga Retreat & Ashram Pass in Goa

Short on time but craving deep rejuvenation? Treat yourself to a full day of wellness, breathwork, ice bath therapy, sunset sound healing, and nourishing organic cuisine in Arambol, Goa.

A Complete Day of Mind-Body Transformation

Our One-Day Ashram Experience is the perfect reset for travelers visiting Goa, digital nomads needing a wellness day, or locals seeking authentic spiritual nourishment.

From 7:00 AM to 8:30 PM, you enjoy unrestricted access to our sanctuary: two full yoga classes, morning breathwork, an exhilarating guided ice bath session, organic Sattvic lunch, swimming pool relaxation, and an evening Tibetan singing bowl sound healing journey.

Day Pass Inclusions

2 Full Yoga Classes

Morning dynamic flow (Hatha/Vinyasa) and evening calming restorative Yin / Kundalini session.

Guided Ice Bath Therapy

Boost circulation, dopamine, and mental grit with our professionally coached cold plunge experience.

Sattvic Organic Lunch

Delicious chef-prepared Ayurvedic meal with fresh salads, curries, grains, and herbal infusion teas.

Sunset Sound Healing

Relax deeply under the palm canopy as Tibetan singing bowls and gongs wash over your nervous system.
