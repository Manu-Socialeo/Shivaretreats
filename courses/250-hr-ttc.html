Multi-Style Mastery

250-Hour Multi-Style Yoga Teacher Training in Goa

Combine the alignment discipline of Hatha, the dynamic power of Ashtanga & Vinyasa, and the somatic restoration of Yin Yoga into one complete 25-day certification.

The Ultimate All-Round Teaching Certification

Why limit your teaching repertoire to a single tradition? The 250-Hour Multi-Style TTC at Shiva Yoga Goa bridges classical roots with modern movement disciplines, allowing graduates to cater to diverse student needs across worldwide studios, wellness retreats, and private clientele.

Enjoy intensive immersion in our beachfront ashram in Arambol with 3 daily Sattvic Ayurvedic meals, comprehensive manual, practical teaching labs, and evening sound healing.

Syllabus Breakdown

4 Core Yoga Traditions

In-depth training in Classical Hatha, Mysore Ashtanga Primary Series, Dynamic Vinyasa, and Restorative Yin.

Hands-on Alignment Clinic

Master safe tactile assists, anatomical modifications, and intelligent prop integration.

Pranayama & Shatkarma

Daily breath purification, bandhas, and yogic kriyas for physical cleansing and mental stamina.

Business of Yoga & Ethics

Studio setup, workshop creation, online teaching strategies, and conscious marketing.
