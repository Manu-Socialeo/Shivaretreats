Yoga Alliance USA RYS 200

200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training Course in Goa

A transformative 21-day residential immersion in Arambol, Goa. Lay the ultimate foundation in traditional Hatha, Ashtanga, Vinyasa flow, Anatomy, and Vedic Philosophy.

About the 200-Hour YTTC Program

Our 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training Course is an internationally accredited intensive certification program designed for practitioners of all levels who desire to deepen their personal practice and gain the authentic skills, knowledge, and confidence to teach yoga worldwide.

Hosted in our peaceful ashram sanctuary in Arambol, Goa, you will study under seasoned Indian yoga masters who embody traditional yogic values while delivering a contemporary, anatomical understanding of body alignment and safety.

Course Curriculum Modules

1. Asana Practice & Alignment

Classical Hatha poses, Ashtanga Vinyasa Primary Series sequencing, transitions, and hands-on posture adjustments.

2. Pranayama & Breathwork

Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Ujjayi, Bhramari, and Bandhas (energy locks) to channel Prana.

3. Yoga Philosophy & Ethics

Patanjali’s Yoga Sutras, 8 Limbs of Ashtanga, Bhagavad Gita wisdom, Karma, and ethical lifestyle of a teacher.

4. Anatomy & Physiology

Musculoskeletal mechanics, joint kinematics, fascia network, injury prevention, and respiratory physiology.

5. Teaching Methodology

Voice projection, cueing precision, sequencing logic, classroom management, and practical teaching practicum.

6. Ayurveda & Cleansings

Shatkarma yogic cleansings (Jala Neti, Kunjal Kriya), Trataka candle meditation, and Ayurvedic Dosha balance.
