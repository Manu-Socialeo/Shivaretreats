Yoga Alliance 500 RYT Pathway

300-Hour Advanced Yoga Teacher Training Course

Take your practice and teaching mastery to the highest level. 28 days of immersive advanced asana, subtle energy anatomy, Kundalini awakening, and holistic yoga therapy in Goa.

Advanced Mastery for Certified Teachers

Designed for graduates of any 200-hour Yoga Teacher Training Course, this intensive 300-hour program allows you to complete your 500-hour Master Yoga Teacher certification (RYT 500) under the guidance of traditional Indian masters in Arambol, Goa.

We explore advanced inversions, arm balances, creative multi-style sequencing, therapeutic modifications, Tantra & Kundalini energy channels (Nadis & Chakras), and profound Yoga Nidra practices.

Advanced Curriculum Pillars

Advanced Asana & Sequencing

Master advanced arm balances, deep hip openers, backbends, and intuitive peak-pose sequencing.

Kundalini & Subtle Body

Comprehensive study of the 7 Chakras, Prana Vayus, Koshas, and transformative Kundalini kriyas.

Biomechanics & Yoga Therapy

Biomechanics of movement, chronic pain alleviation, posture rehab, and therapeutic prop variations.

Advanced Philosophy & Upanishads

In-depth contemplation of the Mandukya, Katha, and Chandogya Upanishads, Samkhya Karika, and Advaita.
