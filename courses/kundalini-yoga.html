Kundalini Yoga & Chakra Awakening Retreat in Goa

Awaken your primal serpent energy (Kundalini Shakti) and purify the 7 energetic vortexes (Chakras) with classical Tantric Kriyas, sacred mantras, and deep meditative absorption in Arambol, Goa.

Unleash Your Dormant Spiritual Power

Kundalini is the latent spiritual energy coiled at the base of the spine (Muladhara Chakra). When systematically activated through breath, posture, locks (Bandhas), and seed sounds (Bija Mantras), it ascends through the central Sushumna Nadi, dissolving energetic blockages and illuminating supreme consciousness.

Under the watchful guidance of master teachers, this retreat offers a grounded, safe, and deeply empowering method of Kundalini awakening suitable for beginners and experienced seekers alike.

Core Energetic Practices

1. The 7 Chakras System

Root to Crown alignment: clearing psychological, emotional, and ancestral blockages in each energy center.

2. Tantric Kriya Yoga

Dynamic repetitive movements synchronized with rapid breathwork to electrify the nervous system.

3. Bija Mantra & Sound Vibration

Vocal sound tuning (LAM, VAM, RAM, YAM, HAM, OM) to harmonize brainwave states and the pineal gland.

4. Bandhas & Mudras

Mula, Uddiyana, and Jalandhara Bandhas (energy locks) to channel prana up the spinal column.
