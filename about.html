About Shiva Yoga Goa

A living ashram community where ancient Vedic wisdom, conscious living, and heartfelt seva create an authentic sanctuary for seekers from every corner of the earth.

The Ashram Story

More Than a Yoga School: A Sacred Home in Goa

Nestled among the swaying coconut groves and tranquil sands of Arambol in North Goa, Shiva Yoga was born from a deep devotion to preserving classical Indian yogic traditions while making them accessible and life-changing for contemporary seekers.

Here, along with rigorous, accredited Yoga Alliance teacher training (RYT 200, 300, 500), you immerse in authentic ashram living: morning Vedic pujas, silent sunrise walks along the Arabian Sea, organic farm-to-table Ayurvedic nourishment, sacred fire ceremonies (Havan), and heartfelt community living.

By choosing Shiva Yoga, you become part of a family. We take complete care of your journey—from airport pick-up and comfortable eco-cottage lodging to all course materials and transformative excursions—allowing you to dedicate your full attention to inner evolution.

The Four Pillars of Shiva Yoga

Authentic Lineage

Rooted in the ancient Vedic and Tantric scriptures, taught with reverence, precision, and humility by master Indian yogis.

Seva & Compassion

Selfless service caring for rescued animals, nurturing native medicinal herb gardens, and supporting local Goan communities.

Global Accreditation

Yoga Alliance USA Registered Yoga School (RYS 200, 300, 500) ensuring your credentials are respected worldwide.

Sattvic Ayurvedic Living

Organic, seasonal vegetarian cuisine designed in harmony with Ayurvedic principles to purify digestion and uplift consciousness.
