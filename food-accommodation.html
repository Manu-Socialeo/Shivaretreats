Ayurvedic Nutrition

3 Daily Organic Sattvic Meals

Food is Medicine (Ahar). Our kitchen prepares fresh, 100% vegetarian, vegan-friendly, and gluten-sensitive meals following classical Ayurvedic food-combining principles.

Every meal is crafted using locally harvested organic fruits, vegetables, lentils, whole grains, and cold-pressed oils. Spices such as turmeric, ginger, cumin, coriander, and cardamom are balanced to ignite digestive fire (Agni) without causing sluggishness or agitation.

  • Breakfast (10:15 AM): Tropical fruits, warm oats porridge, herbal infusions, chia puddings, and fresh coconut water.
  • Lunch (01:15 PM): Ayurvedic Kitchari, vegetable curries, whole wheat chapatis/rice, fresh sprouts, and digestive buttermilk.
  • Dinner (07:00 PM): Light, easily digestible vegetable soups, roasted squash, steamed greens, and herbal detox teas.
