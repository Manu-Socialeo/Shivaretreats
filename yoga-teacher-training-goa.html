Why Yoga Teacher Training in Goa?

Goa is India's most internationally popular yoga destination — combining ancient spiritual tradition with modern infrastructure, beach living, and world-class teachers.

Authentic Indian Lineage

Learn from master teachers trained in traditional Indian gurukul lineages — not Western adaptations. Experience yoga as it was always meant to be taught.

Year-Round Warm Climate

Goa's tropical climate means you practice yoga in natural light, fresh ocean air, and warmth — ideal conditions for deepening flexibility and pranayama.

Global Community

Students from 50+ countries join our Goa TTC every month. This diversity of perspectives enriches your training and creates lifelong international friendships.

Exceptional Value

India offers world-class yoga training at a fraction of Western prices. Your €800 covers 21 days of accommodation, 3 organic meals daily, and full certification.

Yoga Alliance Certified

Our RYT-200 and RYT-300 certifications are globally recognized, allowing you to register with Yoga Alliance USA and teach in studios worldwide.

Arambol Beach Ashram

Live and practice in our serene beachside ashram in Arambol, North Goa — one of India's last truly authentic hippie beach villages with a strong spiritual community.

Yoga TTC in Goa — Frequently Asked Questions

Is Goa good for Yoga Teacher Training?

Yes. Goa — especially Arambol — is India's premier destination for yoga teacher training. The beach town has a long spiritual heritage, internationally certified schools, year-round warm weather, and a global community of practitioners. Arambol in particular has been a yoga hub since the 1970s.

Which is the best yoga school in Goa for TTC?

Shiva Yoga Goa in Arambol is one of the top-rated Yoga Alliance certified schools in Goa, offering 200hr, 300hr, Yin, Prenatal, and Spanish TTC programs. We have trained students from 50+ countries with a 4.9-star average rating.

How much does Yoga Teacher Training in Goa cost?

A 200-hour residential Yoga TTC in Goa typically costs €700–€1,200 all-inclusive (accommodation, meals, training, certificate). At Shiva Yoga Goa, the 200-hour TTC is €800 fully inclusive — excellent value for a month in paradise.

When is the best time to do Yoga TTC in Goa?

The best months for Yoga TTC in Goa are October through April during the dry season. Shiva Yoga offers monthly batches year-round, but October to March provides ideal weather for yoga practice and beach living in Arambol.

Do I need prior yoga experience for a 200-hour TTC?

No prior teaching experience is required, but at least 6 months of personal yoga practice is recommended. Many of our students come with just a love of yoga and leave as confident, certified teachers ready to teach anywhere in the world.
